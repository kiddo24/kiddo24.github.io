Our Story

Nearly four decades of faith in Orange County

St. Mary Romanian Orthodox Church traces its beginnings to March 1985, when Fr. Cornel Avramescu celebrated the parish's first Divine Liturgy at St. Paul's Episcopal Church in Tustin, with the blessing of the Romanian Orthodox Episcopate of America. As the community grew, the parish acquired its own home on South Walnut Street in Anaheim, and the church building was consecrated in 1996.

Today St. Mary's continues that legacy as a welcoming, bilingual community — a parish where longtime Romanian-American families and newer arrivals from Romania worship side by side, and where services are offered in both Romanian and English so that every generation can take part.

We are a parish of the Romanian Orthodox Episcopate of America (ROEA), within the Orthodox Church in America, part of the Pacific Southwest Deanery.

Ministries & Parish Life

Church Choir

Corul Bisericii is our mixed-voice choir of about fifteen singers, blending longtime parishioners and newer arrivals from Romania to lead the congregation in prayerful song at every service.

Church School

Școala Duminicală teaches children the Orthodox faith each Sunday, with room for over fifty students ranging from preschool through high school.

Ladies Auxiliary

Our chapter of ARFORA (the Associated Romanian Orthodox Ladies Auxiliaries of America) supports parish life through fellowship, hospitality, and service — alongside our ushers and festival volunteers.
